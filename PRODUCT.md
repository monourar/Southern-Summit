# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Homeowners** — US property owners with existing land who want a pool sanctuary, outdoor kitchen, or full-yard redesign designed fully remotely. They submit property boundaries, site photos, or drone footage and receive an immersive 3D design they can review via video consultation.
- **Builders & contractors** — local masons, pool builders, and electrical contractors who need dimensioned, buildable CAD packages (hardscape layouts, pool depth profiles, material callouts, lighting line diagrams). The site offers a dedicated "Builder Partnership Track."

## Product Purpose

Southern Summit Outdoor engineers extraordinary outdoor living through photorealistic 3D spatial architecture and contractor-ready CAD master plans, delivered entirely remotely. Success means a homeowner gains a buildable, immersive design without a local landscape architect, and a builder gains a turn-key technical package they can build from directly.

## Positioning

Fully remote outdoor-living design that spans both sides of the build: the homeowner's photorealistic 3D experience and the contractor's construction documentation, produced from one 3D digital twin of the yard built on satellite GIS data plus site photos/drone video. The two-revision-round design process and remote video delivery are the mechanism a nearby landscape studio cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- Delivery pipeline: Discovery → Concept Development → Immersive 3D Design → Final Design Package, reviewed through interactive video consultations.
- Inputs: property boundaries, site photos, drone video clips, satellite GIS data.
- Deliverables: dimensioned hardscape layouts, pool depth profiles, material callout schedules, lighting line diagrams, formatted for local trades.
- Every package includes two structured revision rounds; contractor presentation guidance and quote evaluation are offered.
- Design focus areas offered: full estate master plan, pool & spa sanctuary, outdoor culinary kitchen, builder partnership track.

## Product Principles

1. **Remote-first:** the entire journey — discovery through final package — happens through imagery, video consults, and digital twins; the site must never imply in-person presence.
2. **Dual-audience proof:** homeowners must feel the design and builders must be able to build from it; every claim needs both an experience and a technical proof.
3. **Evidence-backed claims only:** testimonials, names, and figures must reflect real work; never fabricate proof.
4. **Process legibility:** the four-stage pipeline is the core promise and must stay legible across the surface.
5. **Accessibility as standard:** keyboard support, AA contrast, and reduced-motion handling are baseline requirements of this codebase.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The codebase targets WCAG AA contrast (applied in prior work) and honors `prefers-reduced-motion` for the custom cursor; no formal product-specific accessibility standard has been confirmed by the owner beyond this.
